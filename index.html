About us

One team from design to handover

Prime Engineering is an engineering and industrial services firm based in Vatva GIDC, Ahmedabad. The same team designs, fabricates, installs and hands over, so our clients deal with a single point of responsibility.

We have delivered work for 18 companies in India, most of them chemical, agrochemical and process companies, and for the Jakarta Government in Indonesia. Plastic recycling

Continuous pyrolysis: waste plastic to energy

A continuous plant is fed and discharged without stopping, so it runs at steady temperature. We designed, fabricated, installed and now operate one in Jakarta, Indonesia.

Jakarta plastic recycling plant

Continuous pyrolysis plant, waste plastic to energy

Client
Jakarta Government, Indonesia
Capacity
10 tonnes of plastic per day
Our role
Design, fabrication, installation and operation
Status
Running in Jakarta

Built with Mr. Yamaji Masamura of Japan, the inventor of the technology.

  1. Plastic shreddingCuts waste plastic to a uniform size for feeding
  2. Feeding systemFeeds shredded plastic into the reactors continuously
  3. Heating systemSupplies and controls the heat for pyrolysis
  4. Pyrolysis reactorsBreak down waste plastic by heat without oxygen
  5. Condensing systemCondenses the pyrolysis vapours into oil
  6. Waste-to-energy generationUses the products to generate energy
  7. Emission control systemTreats flue gases before release
  8. Carbon discharge systemRemoves the carbon residue from the reactors continuously
